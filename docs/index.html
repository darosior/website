About

I work on Bitcoin at Chaincode Labs, mostly on security-related topics. You can follow my recent work on GitHub, Delving Bitcoin and the bitcoindev mailing list.

I’ve done some security research in the Bitcoin space, and I’m involved in handling vulnerability reports to Bitcoin Core. More recently, I’ve started engaging with how Bitcoin should prepare for a potential cryptographically-relevant quantum computer.

I’ve also worked on Bitcoin’s scripting capabilities. I contributed to Miniscript to make it possible to safely use existing primitives to their full extent, and to BIP 448 to introduce new useful primitives. These can make personal and professional self-custody safer, and help scale Bitcoin by supporting offchain usage with different tradeoffs than existing solutions.

I previously co-founded Wizardsardine, where I remain a shareholder. There I researched Bitcoin vaults and implemented Revault, a Bitcoin vault for multi-party custody. I also started and maintained the popular Liana wallet, which aims to reduce the risk of losing access to funds.

Before that I co-founded Bitcoin-Lyon, a local Bitcoin broker in Lyon, France.

I’m interested in information security, economics and politics.

Publications

  • BIP 448 - Bitcoin soft fork proposal to extend scripting capabilities with simple, well-understood primitives that improve offchain scaling protocols.
  • CVE-2025-62860 - thousands of Bitcoin nodes had their DoS and privacy protections disabled. Companion blog post here.
  • CVE-2025-46598 - Bitcoin Core could be repeatedly stalled for multiple seconds at virtually no cost to an attacker. The same setup cost could be reused to stall multiple nodes in parallel.
  • BIP 54 - Bitcoin soft fork proposal to address four long-standing vulnerabilities and weaknesses.
  • CVE-2024-38365 - chain split vulnerability in btcd which can be exploited at virtually no cost.
  • Ledger Security Bulletin 019 - some spending conditions of a Bitcoin Script were not correctly implemented by the Ledger firmware and could have led to a loss of funds.
  • CVE-2022-32984 - under some configurations the credentials to the Lightning node of a BTCPay Server point of sale were leaked, exposing all its funds to an attacker.
  • Swambo, J., Poinsot, A. (2021). Risk Framework for Bitcoin Custody Operation with the Revault Protocol. - In: Bernhard, M., et al. Financial Cryptography and Data Security. FC 2021 International Workshops.
